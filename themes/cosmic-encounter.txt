# Cosmic Encounter aliens
VIRUS = alien that multiplies its ships by its card
ORACLE = alien that makes its foe play first, face up
ZOMBIE = alien whose ships never go to the warp
CLONE = alien that keeps the card it played
LOSER = alien that can make the lower total win
AMOEBA = alien that oozes ships in or out
MUTANT = alien that refills its hand to eight
HEALER = alien that rescues others' ships from the warp
TRADER = alien that swaps hands
TRIPLER = alien that triples low cards
WARRIOR = alien that toughens with every fight
MIRROR = alien that reverses attack digits
MISER = alien with a hoard of cards
SHADOW = alien that assassinates ships
MACHINE = alien that keeps having encounters
VOID = alien that eradicates ships
FILCH = alien that steals used cards
FIDO = alien that fetches discarded cards
HUMAN = alien that adds four to its total
CHOSEN = alien blessed with divine intervention
MIND = alien that reads your hand
WARP = where lost ships wait
FLARE = card that boosts an alien's power
